This folder contains all necessary to assemble your own RCHub X.

In STL folder you find files to for 3D printed case.
Raspberry Pi 5 board - choose "RPi 5". 
Raspberry Pi 4B board - choose "RPi 4".
Raspberry Pi 3B board - choose "Back RPi 5" and "Spacer Back RPi 4".

Wiring is the same as in case of X306, just much less parts: only 1.44 inch display and 3.5mm AUX socket is required.
